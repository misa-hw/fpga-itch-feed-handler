On-board self-test (QMTech Wukong XC7A100T, 100 Mb/s MII build, 2026-09-30)

Bitstream: SELFTEST_PCAP=build/board_subset.pcap TARGET_MHZ=50 ./fpga/build_board.sh
  build/board_subset.pcap = packets 0-7 and 300-399 of build/board_slow.pcap
  (108 frames: the 8 stock directory packets and all 96 Adds + 1 Delete)
Frames are played from an on-chip ROM 2^20 RX_CLK cycles after reset; no PHY
traffic, cable or network configuration. Programmed with openFPGALoader over
JTAG; UART read at 115200 baud.

Final status line:
S1 frm=0000006c acc=0000006c flt=00000000 err=00000000 fcs=00000000 msg=0000006c gap=00000001 dup=00000000 cmd=00000061 chg=00000027 hash=6debb1d9 lost=00000005 kick=00000000 rfl=00000000 ovf=00000000 ord=0000005f lvl=00000054 map=ff blen=00000000 dord=00000061 fcmd=00000061 fmax=00000001 uns=00000000 bst=01 bfe=1 clk=01a1df12

Model (./_build/default/bin/pcapgen.exe expect build/board_subset.pcap -summary):
chg=00000027 hash=6debb1d9

chg and hash equal the model. cmd=0x61 (97 commands), ord=0x5f, lvl=0x54,
kick=0: identical to RTL and gate-level simulation. The 34 event lines that
the UART printed are an ordered subsequence of the model's 39; lost=5 lines
were dropped by the 31-entry display FIFO because the ROM plays faster than
115200 baud (the hash is computed before that FIFO and covers every change).
